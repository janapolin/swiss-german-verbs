# grammar_rules.md — extracted from the source conjugation sheets

> ALL forms below were transcribed from photographs of a Züritüütsch textbook.
> They are high-confidence but NOT infallible — the project owner (who knows the
> dialect) should spot-check, especially anything tagged `VERIFY`. Treat this file
> as the authority for `ingestion/expand_regular.py` and the rendering engine.

The source separates **regular** verbs (three conjugation classes A/B/C) from the
**most important irregular** verbs (given fully conjugated — see
`verbs_irregular.csv`). Regular present forms are generated by rule; participles are
stored explicitly (the participle morphophonology is too irregular to generate
cleanly — see §3).

---

## 1. Regular present tense — classes A / B / C

The citation form in the source is the **ich-form** (`stem + e`), which is also the
Zdt infinitive (the High German `-n` infinitive ending always drops in Zdt). Derive
the stem by removing the trailing `e` (and, for separable verbs, the leading prefix
first — see §4).

Endings on the stem, by person:

| Class | sg1 (ich) | sg2 (du) | sg3 (er) | pl (mir/ir/si) | When the class applies |
|-------|-----------|----------|----------|----------------|------------------------|
| **A** | `-e`      | `-sch`   | `-t`     | `-ed`          | default                |
| **B** | `-e`      | `-isch`  | `-et`    | `-ed`          | stems needing an epenthetic vowel (the `-i-` in sg2 is inserted "aus lautlichen Gründen"); sg3 takes `-et` |
| **C** | `-e`      | `-isch`  | `-t`     | `-ed`          | **only after `s, sch, ss, tz, z`** |

Worked examples:
- A `mache` (stem `mach`): `mache / machsch / macht / mached`
- B `luege` (stem `lueg`): `luege / luegisch / lueget / lueged`
- C `butze` (stem `butz`): `butze / butzisch / butzt / butzed`

The plural is identical across 1./2./3. person (`-ed`) in every class.

## 2. Auxiliary assignment (`ha` vs `si`) in the perfect

Default is **`ha`**. The source marks a minority that take **`si`** — these are the
usual motion / change-of-state / position verbs. From the sheets, the `si` regular
verbs are:

`bliibe, faare, fale, lauffe (gloffe), räne (grännt), schwüme (gschwume),`
`springe (gsprunge), umcheere (umgcheert), sitze (gsässe), räise (gräist)`

Among irregulars, `si` verbs are: `sii, gaa, choo, staa, ligge, wèèrde`
(`staa` and `ligge` tagged VERIFY in `verbs_irregular.csv`). Everything else is `ha`.

`werde`/`wèèrde` is the **future** auxiliary for all verbs (`wird` + infinitive) and
is also the only verb whose own perfect uses `si` → `isch woorde`.

## 3. Regular participle formation

Base rule: `g + stem + t` (the High German prefix `ge-` is shortened to bare `g-`),
e.g. `mache → gmacht`, `leere → gleert`. BUT the `g-` assimilates to the stem's
initial consonant, and many forms are "strong" (vowel change, marked **bold** in the
source). Observed patterns:

- stem-initial `b` → `p`, no `g`: `bliibe → plibe`, `binde → punde`, `bhalte → phalte`
- stem-initial `d` → `t`, no `g`: `danke → tanket`
- stem-initial voiceless stop `p/t/k` keeps the stop, no `g`: `poschte → poschtet`,
  `troue → trout`, `trucke → truckt`
- stem-initial sibilant `s/z` etc.: often no `g` (`zäige → zäiget`, `passe → passt`)
- stem-initial `ch`: `g + ch` (`choche → gchochet`)
- separable verbs: prefix + the participle, `g-` lands inside (`uufhänke → uufghänkt`)
- inseparable `ver-/be-/er-/ent-`: NO `g-` at all (`verzele → verzelt`,
  `verlüüre → verloore`)

Because of this, **do not generate participles by rule** — store the explicit value.
The participle for every regular verb is in `verbs_regular.csv` (transcribed from the
perfect sheet). Generate only the present by rule.

## 4. Separable prefixes

Prefixes are not marked in the source but come from a finite set; detect by
longest-prefix match on the infinitive, then store `separable_prefix` and strip it
from the stored present forms (the participle and infinitive keep it attached).

Separable prefixes seen / expected:
`aa-, ab-, uuf-, uf-, um-, ii-, uus-, zue-, mit-, wäg-, vor-, abe-, ufe-, dure-, zäme-, nache-`

Inseparable prefixes (NOT separable; no participle `g-`):
`ver-, be-, er-, ent-, ge-, zer-`

Placement (handled by the rendering engine, see CLAUDE.md §6/§7):
- attached in infinitive, participle, future infinitive: `uufstaa`, `uufghänkt`
- detached to clause end in finite present / finite Konjunktiv II: `er hänkt uuf`

## 5. Spelling normalization (confirm exact glyphs before coding)

The source uses two non-standard glyphs that must be normalized on ingestion:

1. **doubled grave-e `èè`** (the owner described this as `éé`): → `ee` word-internal,
   `eh` word-final. Affected words include `fèèle, lèère, gfèèlt, glèèrt, wèèr(de)`
   and the irregular forms `gèè, nèè, ggèè`. (e.g. `lèère → leere`.)
2. **o-with-dot(s)-above `ȯȯ`** (the owner's "triple-umlaut o"): → `ö`. Affected
   words: `rȯȯtle → röötle`, participle `grȯȯtlet/rȯȯtlet → gröötlet/röötlet`.

> VERIFY: confirm the exact Unicode codepoints of these two glyphs against the PNGs,
> and confirm the `èè` vs `éé` question, so `normalize.py` is exact rather than a
> guess. Implement as an ordered `(pattern, replacement, position)` table.

## 6. Konjunktiv II — only six verbs (v1)

The source gives synthetic Konjunktiv II only for these "Höflichkeitsformeln". For
all other verbs, `has_konjunktiv2 = false` and no KII card is generated (no
periphrastic fallback in v1, per project decision).

| Konj II | sii | haa | wele | sele | chöne | wèèrde |
|---------|-----|-----|------|------|-------|--------|
| sg1 ich | wèèr | hett | wett | sett | chönt | wüürd |
| sg2 du  | wèèrsch | hettsch | wettsch | settsch | chöntsch | wüürdsch |
| sg3 er  | wèèr | hett | wett | sett | chönt | wüürd |
| pl      | wèèred | hetted | wetted | setted | chönted | wüürded |

(`sele`'s participle exists "nur mit Konj. II".)
